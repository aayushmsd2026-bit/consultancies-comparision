{SITE_NAME} is an independent place where students rate and review study-abroad consultancies in Nepal.

It answers one question for a student or a parent: **can I trust this consultancy before I pay them?**

## Why it exists

Every year a very large number of Nepali students apply to study abroad. Most go through a consultancy. Most families cannot easily tell a careful consultancy from a careless one until the money is paid.

"Top 10" lists are often advertising. Testimonials on a consultancy's own website are chosen by the consultancy. We wanted one honest, quiet place where real experiences are collected and nobody can pay to look better.

## How we stay independent

- Consultancies cannot pay to change a score, move up a list or remove a review.
- Every ranking uses one public formula. It is explained in the [review policy](/review-policy).
- We read every review before it goes live.
- If we ever show advertising, it will be clearly labelled and kept apart from reviews and rankings.

## Where the registration information comes from

We compare consultancy names with the list published by the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology. Each profile shows the date we last checked.

Lists change and we can make mistakes. The Ministry is the final authority. If you see an error, please tell us.
