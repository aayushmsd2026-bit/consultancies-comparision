We collect as little as we can. This page says exactly what, and why.

## If you only read the site

You do not need an account. We do not use advertising trackers.

We store two small things in your browser:

- your language choice (English or Nepali)
- an unfinished review, if you started one. It stays on your own device until you send it or clear it.

Our hosting companies keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security and to keep the site running.

## If you write a review

When you sign in with Google we receive your **name** and **email address** from Google. We do not receive your password.

We store:

| What | Who can see it |
| --- | --- |
| Your review: ratings, text, country, study level, intake year, outcome and amounts paid | Everyone, after we approve it |
| Your name, shown the way you chose: full name, initials or anonymous | Everyone, after we approve it |
| Your email address | Only our team |
| A proof file, if you send one | Only our team, and only until we have checked it |

We never show your email address or phone number. We never sell your information.

## Proof files

Proof files are stored in a private space. Only you and our team can open them. After we have checked a file, we delete it.

Before you upload, you may cover details that are not needed, such as your passport number.

## If you report a review

We store your report. If you are signed in, we also store which account sent it. We do not show this to anyone outside our team.

## Who helps us run the site

- **Supabase** stores the database, the sign-in and the proof files.
- **Vercel** delivers the website.
- **Google** provides sign-in.

Each of these companies processes data for us under its own terms.

## How long we keep things

- Published reviews stay until you ask us to remove them or we remove them under the [review policy](/review-policy).
- Proof files are deleted after checking.
- Your account stays until you ask us to delete it.

## Your choices

You can ask us to:

- show you what we hold about you
- correct or delete your review
- delete your account

Write to {CONTACT_EMAIL} from the email address you signed in with.

## Children

{SITE_NAME} is meant for students who are applying to study abroad, and for their parents. If you are under 16, please use it with a parent or guardian.

## Changes

If we change this page in an important way, we will say so on the site. The date at the top shows the last change.
