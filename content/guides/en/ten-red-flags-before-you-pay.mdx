Most consultancies do honest work. Some do not. The dishonest ones tend to behave in the same few ways.

If you see one of these signs, slow down and ask more questions. If you see several, walk away.

## 1. "Your visa is guaranteed"

Only an embassy decides a visa. No consultancy can promise one. Anyone who says "100% visa" is either lying or planning something you do not want your name on.

## 2. They will not show their approval letter

A registered consultancy has a letter from the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology. If they change the subject when you ask, that is your answer. You can also [check the name yourself](/check).

## 3. Pressure to pay today

"Only two seats left." "The offer ends this evening." Real deadlines come in writing from a college or an embassy. A rush that only the consultancy can see is a sales trick.

## 4. No receipt

Every payment needs a printed or written receipt with the consultancy's name, stamp, the date and the amount. Paying into a staff member's personal account, or in cash with no receipt, leaves you with no proof.

## 5. The costs keep changing

You were told one amount. Now there is a "processing fee", then a "file charge", then something else. Ask for the full list in writing before you pay the first rupee.

## 6. They offer to "arrange" documents

> Fake bank statements, fake experience letters and fake certificates end careers.

If an embassy finds a false document, the refusal is in your name, not the consultancy's. It can block you from that country for years. Never agree to this, even if they say everyone does it.

## 7. They want to keep your originals

Your passport and original certificates are yours. A consultancy needs copies for most steps. Holding your originals is a way to stop you from leaving.

## 8. Only one college, every time

If every student is sent to the same college, ask why. The advice may be shaped by commission and not by your marks, budget or goals.

## 9. Nothing in writing

Promises made only by voice are easy to deny later. Ask for the service agreement and the refund rules on paper. Read them at home before you sign.

## 10. They get angry when you ask questions

You are about to spend a large amount of your family's money. Asking questions is normal. A consultancy that is annoyed by careful students prefers careless ones.

## What to do if something has gone wrong

- Keep every receipt, message and paper.
- Ask the consultancy for a written answer.
- If you think you were cheated, you can complain to the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology, and to the police.
