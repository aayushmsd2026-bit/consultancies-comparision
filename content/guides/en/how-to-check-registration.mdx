Before you pay a consultancy, check that it is registered. It takes a few minutes and it is the cheapest protection you have.

## Way 1: Use our checker

Type the consultancy's name into our [registration checker](/check). We compare it with the list from the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology.

You will see one of four answers:

| Answer | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Registered** | The name is on the list we hold. |
| **Not on the list** | We could not find it. Ask for the approval letter. |
| **Expired** | It was on the list before. Its approval is not current. |
| **Not checked** | We have not compared this one with the list yet. |

Every answer shows the date we last checked. Lists change, so look at that date.

## Way 2: Ask for the approval letter

Ask the consultancy to show you its approval letter from the Ministry. Look at three things:

<Checklist>

- The name on the letter matches the name on the signboard and on your receipt
- The address matches the office you are standing in
- The letter is current, not an old one

</Checklist>

A branch office should be able to show that the branch itself is approved, not only the head office in another city.

## Way 3: Check with the Ministry

The Ministry of Education, Science and Technology publishes notices and lists of approved consultancies. If you are unsure, the Ministry is the final word, not us and not the consultancy.

## Names can be confusing

Many consultancies have similar names. Some use a short name on the signboard and a long legal name on paper. When you check:

- Use the full legal name from a receipt or the approval letter.
- Try a shorter part of the name if nothing is found.
- Compare the address as well as the name.

## What registration does not tell you

> Registered means allowed to operate. It does not mean good.

A registered consultancy can still be slow, careless or expensive. An unregistered one has no permission to do this work at all.

So do both: check the registration, then [read what students say](/consultancies).
